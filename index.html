GLITCH WORLD

2D ADVENTURE + PUZZLE + ACTION

DEVELOPED BY:
ROHITH & SUNRUTHI

Glitch World is an offline browser-based 2D adventure game where players explore hazardous levels, solve puzzles, activate cyber glitch energy, engage tactical AI soldiers, and defeat the fortress commander.

  • ◆ Pure HTML5 / Canvas 2D Engine
  • ◆ Glitch Ability & Wall Phasing
  • ◆ Dynamic AI Soldiers with Vision
  • ◆ Mini-Boss with 4 Attack Modes
  • ◆ Web Audio Synthesizer & Tracks
  • ◆ Local Checkpoint & Save System
  • ◆ Responsive Touch & PC Controls
  • ◆ Zero External APIs / 100% Offline
